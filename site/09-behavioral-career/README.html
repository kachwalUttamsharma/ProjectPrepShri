Behavioral and Career Track (interview-focused, ~4 weeks core, then ongoing)

Goal: pass the non-coding parts of SWE and forward-deployed-engineer (FDE) loops: behavioral rounds, customer scenarios, process strategy, resume/portfolio, and clear communication while coding. Reading plus out-loud practice; no code. Anything about specific companies' processes or values is generic: verify against official sources and your recruiter.

Study order

Order File Topics Time
1 01-behavioral-fundamentals.md What is assessed, STAR/CAR, story bank, questions by theme 2 weeks (start stories early)
2 02-fde-customer-scenarios.md FDE role, 15+ scenarios, discovery/scoping/demo/post-mortem templates 1-2 weeks
3 05-communication-in-technical-interviews.md Thinking aloud, hints, recovering, testing verbally read once, apply to every coding session
4 04-resume-and-portfolio.md Resume, bullets, GitHub portfolio, LinkedIn do before applying (month 5-6)
5 03-interview-process-and-strategy.md Loop structure, timeline, mocks, negotiation read early, revisit before applying
6 06-master-study-schedule.md 12-month plan across every folder read first, reference weekly

Suggested real order: skim 06 and 03 on day 1 (orientation), then 01, 05, 02, 04.

Weekly routine for this track (about 1-1.5 hrs/week inside the 7-8 hr budget, more near applying)

  1. Write or refine one story (20 min).
  2. Say two behavioral answers out loud, recorded on your phone (20 min); listen back.
  3. One FDE scenario out loud (15 min).
  4. Monthly: one full mock with a human (see 03).

Tips

  • Stories come from real experience. Never fabricate; interviewers probe with follow-ups until the details run out.
  • Speak in "I", not "we": say what you did, then credit the team.
  • Quantify: time saved, errors reduced, users affected. If you lack numbers, give honest estimates and say they are estimates.
  • Career-changer or student without work history: use projects, internships, open source, volunteering, coursework, and the course projects in this repo.
  • Company values, loop formats, and levels change. Treat every company-specific statement in these files as a prompt to check, not a fact.
