06 - Master Study Schedule (~12 months, 7-8 hrs/week)

Folders: 01-basics, 02-oop, 03-dsa, 04-fastapi, 05-fde-projects, 06-sql, 07-aws, 08-system-design, 09-behavioral-career (this folder), 10-python-depth, 11-lld, 12-cs-fundamentals, 13-llm-ai-concepts. (10-13 are being written in parallel; refer to them by folder name and check their READMEs when ready.)

This is a guide; adjust speed to your pace. If you fall behind, extend a phase rather than skipping checkpoints. Total about 52 weeks x 7-8 hrs = roughly 380 hours.

Weekly template (7-8 hrs)

Block Hours Activity
A 3 Main track lesson/project of the phase
B 2-2.5 DSA practice (03-dsa), timed, from month 2 onward
C 1 Secondary track (SQL, CS fundamentals, Python depth)
D 1 Behavioral/communication/FDE (09) or review/flashcards
E 0.5 Weekly review: what I learned, what is shaky, plan next week
Phase 1 (months 1-2) leans heavier on A; phases 4-5 increase B and mocks.

Phase 1 - Foundations (weeks 1-8, months 1-2)

  • Folders: 01-basics, 02-oop, 10-python-depth (light), start 03-dsa (arrays, hashing, strings).
  • Weekly: 4 hrs 01/02, 2 hrs problem solving, 1 hr reading 12-cs-fundamentals basics, 0.5 review.
  • 09: read README and 06, 03 once (orientation, 1 hr total).
  • Milestone: write small programs without tutorials; explain classes, iterators, exceptions; solve easy problems in 20 min.
  • Checkpoint (week 8): build a CLI app with tests; solve 25+ easy problems.

Phase 2 - Core engineering (weeks 9-18, months 3-4)

  • Folders: 03-dsa (core: two pointers, sliding window, linked lists, stacks/queues, trees, recursion), 06-sql, 04-fastapi start, 10-python-depth, 12-cs-fundamentals (OS, networking, memory).
  • Weekly: 2.5 hrs 03-dsa, 2 hrs 06-sql then 04-fastapi, 1.5 hrs 10/12, 1 hr 09 (start story bank: write 3 stories).
  • Milestone: solve medium problems in 30-35 min in a common pattern; write joins/window functions/CTEs; FastAPI CRUD with a database.
  • Checkpoint (week 18): 80+ problems solved total; SQL fluent on mixed queries; ticket API with tests running.

Phase 3 - FDE projects and AI (weeks 19-28, months 5-6)

  • Folders: 04-fastapi (auth, testing, deployment), 05-fde-projects (ticket API, RAG demo, agent, eval harness), 13-llm-ai-concepts, 03-dsa (graphs, heaps, binary search, intro DP).
  • Weekly: 3 hrs 05/13 projects, 2 hrs 03-dsa, 1 hr 11-lld start or 12, 1 hr 09 (finish 8-10 stories; read 02).
  • Milestone: 4 portfolio repos with READMEs; story bank complete; one FDE scenario answered fluently per week.
  • Checkpoint (week 28): resume drafted (see 04); GitHub profile polished; 130+ problems solved.
  • Start: solo timed practice; first friend mock behavioral (month 6).

Phase 4 - Cloud, design, depth (weeks 29-40, months 7-9)

  • Folders: 07-aws (6 weeks), 08-system-design (6 weeks, can overlap alternating), 11-lld, 12-cs-fundamentals, 03-dsa (DP, backtracking, advanced graphs, review).
  • Weekly: 3 hrs 07/08 alternating, 2 hrs 03-dsa (mixed random mediums), 1 hr 11-lld, 1 hr 09 (communication drills from 05, scenario practice).
  • Milestone: design 5+ classic systems in 40 min; deploy a project to AWS; do OOP design in 35 min.
  • Checkpoint (week 40): 200+ problems; 8+ mocks completed (cadence in 03); resume reviewed by an industry person.
  • Start: weekly mock interviews (coding/design/behavioral rotating).

Phase 5 - Interview readiness and applying (weeks 41-52, months 10-12)

  • Folders: all review. Priority: 03-dsa timed mixed sets, 08-system-design, 11-lld, 02/01 in 09, 13-llm-ai-concepts review, 10-python-depth gotchas.
  • Weekly: 3 hrs DSA timed, 1.5 hrs design, 1 hr behavioral/FDE, 1-1.5 hrs mock interviews, 1 hr review of weak areas.
  • Applying: begin tier-3 applications and referrals at about week 36-40 (month 9) so early interviews serve as practice; apply to tier 1-2 about week 44+ once consistent in mocks. Cluster active processes.
  • Milestone: pass 80%+ of mock coding rounds with narration; two full-loop simulations done; negotiation notes prepared (03).
  • Checkpoint (week 52): reflect on offers/feedback; adjust plan for another cycle if needed.

When to start what

Activity Start
Story bank writing Month 3
Timed DSA practice Month 3-4
Friend mock (behavioral) Month 6
Weekly mocks Month 7-8
Resume polish Month 6
Tier-3 applications and networking Month 8-9
Tier-1/2 applications Month 10-11, when mocks consistently pass
Treat these as guidance; if ready earlier, start earlier. Real interviews are the best practice but a burned application can have a re-application cooldown (verify per company), so do not rush dream companies.

Monthly review (30 minutes)

  1. Count hours, problems, mocks done versus plan.
  2. List 3 weakest topics; schedule them.
  3. Update the story bank and project READMEs.
  4. Adjust the next month; protect rest days. Burnout ends plans faster than slow progress.

Tips

  • Consistency beats intensity: 4 x 2 hrs beats one 8-hr day.
  • Keep a mistakes log (problems failed, why, the pattern missed) and revisit weekly.
  • Protect your projects: finish and document them; they are your evidence.
  • Verify all company-specific process claims with official sources before relying on them.
