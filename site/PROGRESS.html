How to work

  1. Open the week's lesson.md, read it, and type the examples into the REPL.
  2. Fill in exercises.py (replace each ...).
  3. Run that week's tests from this folder (one week at a time): .venv/bin/pytest 01-basics/week-01-first-steps -v
